# Live demo

This is the [web page](web-page.md) that runs on your printer, here against a pretend printer.
It prints, cools the bed, clears it and starts the next job by itself. Nothing is sent to a real
printer.

Try **Eject now**, the **Print queue**, **Check a file** or the **Settings**. Switch to Arabic or
to the light theme at the top of the page.

[Open it full screen](live/web/index.html?demo){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" }
[Start with an eject](live/web/index.html?demo=eject){ .md-button target="_blank" }
